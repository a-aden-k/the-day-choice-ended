The Day Choice Ended

A Story of Betrayal, Injustice, Truth and the Price of Survival

By Abdullahi Sheikh Aden (Karieson)

At 8:30 a.m. on Hidar 29, 2002, a prisoner rides toward the courthouse. What awaits him inside may decide more than his freedom—it may decide whether truth still has a place in a world ruled by power.

Ibrahim’s story unfolds against the streets of Addis Ababa and the shadow of a courtroom, where a man’s future hangs in the balance. Once driven by hope and the belief that hard work and integrity could shape his destiny, he now faces a moment that threatens everything he thought he knew about justice.

As accusations and betrayal close in, the courtroom becomes the centre of a struggle over truth, responsibility, and the choices that lead a life to the edge. Ibrahim must face not only the judgment before him, but the painful question of how much control a person truly has when powerful forces begin deciding his fate.

The Day Choice Ended is a gripping story of injustice, resilience, and the cost of survival. When the truth finally comes to light, will it be enough to restore what has been taken—or will the most important choice still lie ahead?
